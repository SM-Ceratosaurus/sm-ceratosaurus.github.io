Guess The Word

Use your keyboard to enter a five-letter word guess and press enter. If the color is greyed out, the letter isn't used in the secret word. If the color is yellow, it is used but is in the wrong position. If the color is green, it is the correct letter and position. You have up to 6 guesses to get it right, good luck!
